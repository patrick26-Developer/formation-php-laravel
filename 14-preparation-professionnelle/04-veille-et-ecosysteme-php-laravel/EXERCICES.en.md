# Exercises — 14.4 Technology Watch and the PHP/Laravel Ecosystem

## Exercise 1 — Checking supported versions (easy)

Check the official PHP supported-versions page and Laravel's. Note the end-of-support dates for the version used by this training (PHP 8.3, Laravel 11+).

## Exercise 2 — Auditing a package before adoption (easy)

Choose a Composer package used in one of this training's projects (for example `dompdf/dompdf` or `guzzlehttp/guzzle`, level 12). On Packagist and GitHub, check: the last release date, the number of installs, open issues. Conclude whether you'd consider it adoptable for a new project today.

## Exercise 3 — Building your personal watch list (medium)

Choose 3 sources from the lesson (or others you know of) suited to your lifestyle, and explain in 1-2 sentences why each one works for you (frequency, format, depth level).

## Exercise 4 — Comparing Livewire and Inertia (medium)

Check Inertia.js's documentation. Write a comparison table with Livewire (module 10) across 4 criteria: frontend language, learning curve, typical use case, dependency on a JS framework.

## Exercise 5 — Planning your next steps (hard)

Based on your results in the level 00 through 14 exercises, write a personal 3-month plan: which modules to review first, which level 13 large project to deepen or extend, and which technology from the "Where to go after this training" section to explore first — with a justification for each choice.
